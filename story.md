# STORY — ORIGINAL DARK FANTASY

## Core premise
The kingdom of Veyr survives beneath a sky trapped between evening and dawn. Its rulers built a network to preserve memories against the Quiet Tide. It saves some people from being forgotten, but steals identity, distorts history and demands a cost.

The Bellbearer wakes beside an iron bell and a name-tag with no name. The bell reveals traces of suppressed memories, not objective truth. The player must compare fragments with people, places and records.

## Central question
Is preserving every memory worth the suffering caused by the system? The player chooses what should be restored, released or entrusted to communities.

## Acts
1. **Ossuary Road:** tutorial, first enemy, sealed burial road.
2. **Village of Unfinished Things:** survivors disagree about a disaster; first meaningful choice.
3. **Forest of Borrowed Faces:** find a missing witness; learn the Quiet Tide imitates intention.
4. **City Beneath the Bell:** factions compete over the network.
5. **Observatory of Ash:** reconstruct the founding event from incompatible records.
6. **Space Between:** confront the network's origin and decide its future.

## Founding history
Queen Aveline wanted to preserve people lost to catastrophe. Astronomer Ilyan helped design an instrument that stores fragments in memory-glass. The crown's pact expanded beyond its limits, confusing remembrance with continuation. The Hollow Regent maintains the system because it has saved lives, but treats continuation as the only responsible option.

## Endings
- **Preservation:** continue the network under new stewardship; stability with ongoing costs.
- **Release:** dismantle it and accept grief and uncertainty.
- **Renewal:** create a limited community-controlled archive based on consent.

## Rules
Memory fragments are partial and potentially biased. The Quiet Tide is not an explanation for everything. The protagonist is not a prophesied savior. Major revelations need earlier clues. Consequences should follow quests and decisions.
