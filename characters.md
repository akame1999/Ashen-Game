# CHARACTER BIBLE

## The Bellbearer — player character
A wanderer with no reliable name and an iron bell tied to the wrist. Notices what others ignore. Begins by seeking a stable past, then learns to choose what should be remembered and what should be released. Not a prophesied savior or secret monarch.

## Sister Maelin — Keeper of Small Names
A guide and archivist in travel-worn robes, carrying wooden name-tags. Believes ordinary people deserve careful remembrance. She erased a dying prisoner's identity to hide a refuge; the prisoner was her brother, and she cannot remember his face. Learns that preserving records is not the same as caring for people.

## Orren Vale — Disgraced Warden
A combat mentor wearing mismatched repaired armor. Believes discipline prevents panic. He obeyed an order to seal a village whose inhabitants shared memories, and still carries the gate key. Must decide whether responsibility requires confession, repair, or both.

## Neme — The Glasswright
A masked craftsperson who upgrades equipment in exchange for recovered memories. Has helped every faction hide something. Must face the difference between understanding a cost and having the right to impose it.

## The Hollow Regent
Final guardian of the old monarchy, wearing a crown-shaped mask and ceremonial armor. Believes the system must continue because it has saved lives. Understands its risks but treats continuation as the only responsible choice. The player must answer the Regent's argument as well as defeat it.

## Ilyan — The Missing Witness
Astronomer and partner to Aveline; helped design the original instrument. His fragmented testimony connects the founding event to the present. Reveal his role through multiple records, not a single exposition dump.

## Queen Aveline
Founder of the memory network. Her motive began as grief and protection, but the system outgrew its purpose. Avoid depicting her as purely evil or purely benevolent.

## Writing rules
Give each character a concrete desire, fear, contradiction and change. Use distinct voices and physical behavior. Do not let every character explain the lore. Record continuity changes in `decisions.md`.
