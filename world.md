# WORLD BIBLE

## Kingdom of Veyr
A basin ringed by mountains like the ribs of a buried giant. The horizon glows amber and the sun never appears as a complete disc. Roads follow the path of a star no longer visible. Communities still need food, water, shelter, work and safe routes.

## Cosmology
- **Quiet Tide:** natural pull of unfinished experience toward silence, distorted by a fracture beneath Veyr.
- **Unremembered:** people partly severed from their histories; not all are enemies.
- **Memory-glass:** mineral preserving a scene, voice or sensation, never an entire person.
- **Bell Between:** iron bell reveals traces of suppressed memory, not certainty.
- **Last Dawn:** boundary of change and acceptance.

## Regions
- **Ossuary Road:** burial complex and tutorial; hanging name-tags and water through stone.
- **Village of Unfinished Things:** hidden settlement and early hub; conflicting memories.
- **Forest of Borrowed Faces:** shifting paths, misleading voices, ambushes and puzzles.
- **City Beneath the Bell:** abandoned capital with bells, water channels, gates and Crownwarden machinery.
- **Observatory of Ash:** origin of the instrument; contradictory evidence.
- **Space Between:** late-game memory geography, uncanny but navigable.

## Environmental storytelling
A barricade built from within, two cups and one set of footprints, a bell with its clapper removed, or a memorial with scraped names can imply stories without spelling them out.

## Consistency
Memory effects have costs. The network has physical consequences: power, maintenance, labor and hierarchy. Symbols keep consistent meanings unless a reveal explains the change. Travel connections must match the world map.
