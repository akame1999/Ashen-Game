# FACTIONS

## Crownwardens
Maintain gates, bells and memory-glass infrastructure. Value order and fear collapse. Some protect civilians; others enforce cruel containment.

## Keepers of Small Names
Preserve local names, testimony and ordinary histories. Oppose the crown's monopoly but disagree about destroying dangerous records.

## Glasswright Compact
Craftspeople who turn memory-glass into tools and upgrades. Value practical knowledge, but memory extraction creates ethical conflicts.

## The Unmoored
People affected by the Quiet Tide. Not a single enemy faction: some seek release, some recover identity, others exploit confusion.

## Design rules
Every faction has a legitimate need and a harmful blind spot. Show consequences for ordinary people. Members should not all be morally identical. Reputation should affect access, dialogue or resources, and major alliance changes must be recorded in quest notes.
