Ashen Veil — Changelog
======================

Side-scrolling Metroidvania conversion
- Replaced the top-down arena with a side-view, camera-following platformer.
- Added horizontal acceleration, jump buffering, coyote-time jumping, double jump, gravity, and platform landing/ceiling collision.
- Added a long connected route through four dark-fantasy regions, with raised platforms, gaps, shrines/checkpoints, and a final Grave Warden encounter.
- Reworked melee hit checks around a side-view weapon reach and vertical range.
- Added side-view enemy silhouettes, attack telegraphs, ranged Ash Archers, and a boss health display.
- Updated HUD, route progress, controls, and title text for Metroidvania-style play.
- Buffered light/heavy actions through attack recovery and improved rendering layer reuse.
- Added edge-aware enemy movement to reduce enemies walking into pits.

Enhanced combat sandbox (previous top-down prototype)
- Added three weapon archetypes, dodge invulnerability, skills, Soul Burst, enemy waves, boss UI, and combat effects.

Note: this is an early prototype. Runtime behavior should be tested on Windows hardware; it is not a finished commercial game.
