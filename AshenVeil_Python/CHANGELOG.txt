Ashen Veil — Changelog
======================

Illustrated region art
- Added four original SVG background illustrations for the Drowned March, Ashen Chapel, Cinder Bastion and Veiled Throne.
- Integrated region backgrounds with slow parallax movement and region color grading.
- Added a gradual transition blend near region boundaries.
- Layered reusable fog and drifting ember particles over the illustrated scenery.
- Enlarged rendered player and enemy silhouettes around their feet for better readability without changing physics or hitbox dimensions.
- Added a robust fallback to the procedural backgrounds if SVG loading fails on a particular SDL_image build.
- Added a CI assertion that all four region illustrations load under pygame-ce.

Graphics polish and crash fix
- Fixed the Soul Burst charge reward crash: enemy kills update the player's soul charge and clamp to the player's maximum.
- Improved layered parallax scenery, castle silhouettes, moon details, platform masonry, cracks, moss, pillars, torchlight, player/enemy silhouettes, curved attack trails, and rendering surface caching.

Side-scrolling Metroidvania conversion
- Replaced the top-down arena with a side-view, camera-following platformer.
- Added horizontal acceleration, jump buffering, coyote-time jumping, double jump, gravity and platform collision.
- Added a connected route through four dark-fantasy regions, raised platforms, gaps, shrines/checkpoints and a Grave Warden boss.
- Reworked melee hit checks for side-view reach and vertical range.
- Added Hollow enemies, ranged Ash Archers, attack telegraphs and a boss health display.
- Buffered light/heavy actions through attack recovery.

Note: this is an early prototype. Runtime behavior and visual quality should be tested on Windows hardware; it is not a finished commercial game.
