Ashen Veil — Changelog
======================

Graphics polish and crash fix
- Fixed the Soul Burst charge reward crash: enemy kills now update the player's soul charge and clamp to the player's own maximum.
- Added layered parallax mountain ridges, a cratered veil moon, distant stone bridges, star glints and more detailed castle silhouettes with lit windows and broken battlements.
- Reworked platform rendering with shaded masonry courses, chipped edges, deterministic cracks, moss, debris and root details.
- Improved checkpoint shrine silhouettes, torchlight, flames, and low fog. Fog is cached instead of rebuilt in every frame.
- Cached glow stamps and rounded HUD panel surfaces, and corrected alpha blending to reduce temporary allocations and bright edge artifacts.
- Reworked player drawing with split boots, an animated tattered cloak, armor plates, visor highlights, and shaped weapons.
- Reworked hollow, archer and Grave Warden silhouettes with clearer attack telegraphs, glowing eyes and stronger health-bar contrast.
- Added layered curved weapon trails and more consistent warm highlights.

Side-scrolling Metroidvania conversion
- Replaced the top-down arena with a side-view, camera-following platformer.
- Added horizontal acceleration, jump buffering, coyote-time jumping, double jump, gravity, and platform landing/ceiling collision.
- Added a long connected route through four dark-fantasy regions, raised platforms, gaps, shrines/checkpoints, and a final Grave Warden encounter.
- Reworked melee hit checks around side-view weapon reach and vertical range.
- Added side-view enemies, attack telegraphs, ranged Ash Archers, a boss health display, updated HUD and route progress.
- Buffered light/heavy actions through attack recovery and improved rendering layer reuse.

Earlier prototype
- Added three weapon archetypes, dodge invulnerability, weapon skills, Soul Burst, enemy waves, boss UI, and combat effects.

Note: this is an early prototype. Runtime behavior should be tested on Windows hardware; it is not a finished commercial game.
